About Us

Rare is a community archive of the hard-to-find: photographs, footage and written notes that might otherwise disappear. It works a little like Wikipedia — it is open, it is explained for newcomers, and anyone can contribute.

Our idea

Most rare things are not lost all at once. They fade — a negative in an attic, a demo tape in a drawer, a set of notes in a family file. Rare gives them a home with a short, honest description: what it is, where it came from, and why it is worth keeping.

We publish responsibly. Material is shared for study and remembrance, and rights stay with the original owners. If something is yours and you would like it changed or removed, write to us and we will act.

How to contribute

There is no application and no account to create. Send a photo, a video link or a set of notes with a sentence or two of context. If it is rare and it is yours to share, it can appear in the archive — credited however you wish.
